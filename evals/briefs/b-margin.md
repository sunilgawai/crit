# Brief B — Margin

**What it is:** a slow book club. A small group of friends reads the same book at their own pace, and each person shares one highlighted passage a day, with a line about why it stayed with them.

**Who:** adult readers (25–55) who used to be in a book club and miss it, but can't make a monthly meeting. Groups of 3–8 friends.

**Core jobs**
1. Today: see the passages your friends shared, at or before your own page (no spoilers past where you are).
2. Share today's passage: pick a quote (typed or from a photo of the page), add one line.
3. Track where everyone is in the book, and pick the next book together.

**Money:** free for one group and one book at a time. "Margin Club" is €3.99 a month with a 7-day free trial: unlimited groups, the full archive of past books and passages, photo-to-quote.

**Tone the founders used:** "quiet", "literary but not snobby", "like a note passed in class".

**Screens needed:** onboarding (2), today (home), a passage detail with replies, share-a-passage sheet, the group's reading progress, paywall.
