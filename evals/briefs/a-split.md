# Brief A — Split

**What it is:** an app for people who share a flat (typically 3–5 housemates) to keep shared money and chores fair without awkward conversations.

**Who:** housemates in their twenties and early thirties: students, young professionals. One person usually ends up "the one who chases everyone"; the app should take that job off them.

**Core jobs**
1. See at a glance who owes whom, and settle up (weekly, every Sunday).
2. Add a shared bill (rent, energy, internet, groceries) and split it: evenly, by room size or custom.
3. Keep a fair rota of chores (bins, bathroom, kitchen) that rotates on its own.

**Money:** free for one flat. "Split Plus" is €24.99 a year with a 7-day free trial: recurring bills auto-split, bank-import of shared payments, unlimited flats.

**Tone the founders used:** "fair", "no drama", "like a good housemate".

**Screens needed:** onboarding (2), home, a bill detail, chores, add-a-bill sheet, paywall, a success moment after settling up.
